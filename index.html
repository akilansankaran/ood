Out of Distribution

The individuals shaping modern artificial intelligence, and its downstream effects, form an extremely small set that is often disjoint from Harvard and MIT undergraduates. The reason for this effect is a function of proximity, more so than ability.

Thus, this discussion series provides sustained exposure to an elite set of talented individuals at the frontier of intelligence research in their respective fields, compressing the time it takes to orient toward the most impactful vectors of knowledge.

Learning novel ideas from such individuals and contending with them ought to be distinguished, and are treated separately in this discussion series through two lenses of discussion: prior and posterior.

Exposure provides a prior, then updated by argument.

p(θ) → p(θ | frontier insight)

Prior

A speaker sets out their vision of frontier research in the field, with intense recency.

Posterior

A smaller-group discussion, with targeted feedback.

Combining ideas bridges prior and posterior knowledge in a manner that exceeds the sums of components.

Speakers are chosen for their involvement in relevant research areas or dissemination of valuable intellectual insights, from inference-serving to policymaking. Conversations are designed to be maximally accessible.
